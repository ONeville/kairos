02 / Features

Built for the people who run it

Kairos is opinionated about one thing: whoever is pressing the keys should never have to explain a mistake to the congregation.

Two outputs, one operator

The sanctuary projector and the stage confidence monitor are separate windows. The room gets lyrics; the band gets chords, the slide that is up next and the running service clock.

Three zones, no accidents

Search, preview and live stay apart. Everything you type and click lands in the library or the preview pane, and only Take puts it on the screen.

Jumps for a leader who changes the plan

Press 0 for the chorus, 1 to 9 for numbered verses, arrows to step through the arrangement. It works from the live pane while the song is already on screen.

Songs and scripture, entirely offline

1,696 worship songs and the full King James Bible, searchable by any line of text with no internet. Import ChordPro or plain-text lyrics and Kairos finds the duplicates in your library.

A schedule drawn to scale

Enter the planned minutes and every block is drawn at that width against your finish time. Duplicate last Sunday in one click, or keep templates for midweek and youth.

A phone as the remote

Scan a QR code and any phone or tablet on the church Wi-Fi becomes a controller or a read-only stage monitor. Nothing to install and no account to make.

Panic keys that always answer

B blacks the screen out, C clears the words and holds the background, L puts the church logo up. One key each, no menus, no mouse.

A usage log for copyright reporting

Every song that goes live is logged with its author and timestamp. Export a CSV when the reporting comes round, and Kairos keeps a daily snapshot of your library.

03 / How it works

Three moves, every Sunday

There is no mode to remember and no settings screen standing between you and the next song.

01

Plan the order

Start from last Sunday or a template, then set the songs, readings and the time you want to finish. Every item is drawn at the width of its planned minutes, so you can see where the time is going.

02

Stage it privately

Type a title, a line of a lyric or a reference like John 3:16. Click the section you want and it loads into Preview, where only you can see it.

03

Take it live

Press Take when the band reaches the line. Whatever was live drops back into Preview, so going back a slide is one press rather than another search.

06 / FAQ

Common questions before you switch

How does the dual display work?

Kairos opens a separate window for the sanctuary projector and one for the stage monitor. You pick which physical screen each one uses, and the app remembers it and reopens full screen.

Do I need internet on Sunday morning?

No. Songs, Bibles, plans and settings live on your computer. A Wi-Fi dropout cannot stop the service.

Can the band use a phone or tablet as a remote?

Yes. Anyone on the church Wi-Fi scans a QR code and opens the remote in the browser, either as a controller or as a read-only stage monitor.

Can a new volunteer run it without training?

Yes. Searching never touches the screen, the preview is private, and one press on Space sends the slide live.

How does copyright reporting work?

Every song taken live is written to a usage log. When the reporting comes round, export the CSV.

Can I import ChordPro and text files?

Yes. Chords, sections and duplicates are detected automatically during import.

Which operating systems are supported?

Windows 10/11 and macOS, Apple Silicon and Intel. A Linux build follows later.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once for the licence, and minor updates and patches are free.
