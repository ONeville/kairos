Operator Guide · User Manual

Master Kairos in under 5 minutes.

A complete reference guide for AV directors, pastors, and Sunday morning volunteers. Select a topic below to review step-by-step operating workflows.

Pre-Service Planning & Scheduling

Build, balance, and pace your service running order before rehearsal starts.

STEP 1.1

Create or Duplicate Service

Click Services > New Service or hit Duplicate Last Sunday. Kairos copies the order of service structure (welcome loops, sermon placeholder, announcements), letting you focus on updating song sets and scriptures.

STEP 1.2

Set Target Time & Duration

Define your target service start (e.g. 10:00 AM) and planned length (e.g. 75 min). The proportional ribbon draws each item to scale, instantly calculating whether you have spare minutes or risk running late.

STEP 1.3

Add Songs & Passages

Search any song or scripture passage in the library, and click + Schedule to place it in the order. Drag schedule blocks to reorder anytime.

The Sunday Live Switcher Workflow

How volunteers run the service with absolute confidence and zero accidental screen glitches.

STEP 2.1

Search Privately

Type a song title, lyric fragment, or scripture reference (e.g. John 3:16). The library updates instantly. The congregation never sees what you are typing or selecting.

STEP 2.2

Stage in Preview

Click on any section (Verse 1, Chorus, Verse 2) in the parts browser. The slide loads immediately in the Preview pane with full formatting for private operator review.

STEP 2.3

Take to Live

When the vocalists arrive at the lyric, press the big TAKE button or hit Space. The slide transitions to Live. The previous slide remains staged in Preview if you need to bounce back.

Multi-Window Sanctuary & Stage Display Setup

Connecting and configuring secondary screens for congregation and musicians.

STEP 4.1

Connect Physical Displays

Connect your Sanctuary projector via HDMI/DisplayPort and your stage confidence TV via secondary output. In Windows or macOS settings, configure displays in Extend mode (not Mirror).

STEP 4.2

Assign Output Windows

Open Settings > Displays & Output in Kairos. Assign Projector Window to Display 2, and Presenter Window to Display 3. Press F inside either window to snap to full screen.

STEP 4.3

Customize Confidence Cues

The Presenter view automatically renders upcoming next-slide lyrics, musical chords (from ChordPro imports), and the live service time. Musicians never lose their place.

Wireless Mobile Remote Pairing

Control slides from an iPad on a music stand or check stage cues from an iPhone.

STEP 5.1

Enable Remote on Local Wi-Fi

In Kairos, open Settings > Remote and toggle "Allow phones on this network". Kairos enables immediate wireless connectivity across your sanctuary network.

STEP 5.2

Scan Pairing QR Code

Scan the on-screen QR code using any smartphone or tablet camera. The browser opens the Kairos web remote instantly. No App Store download or account registration required.

STEP 5.3

Role-Based Access

Generate a Controller Token (KRS-XXXX) for AV leaders to advance slides and trigger Blackout, or a Viewer Token (KRV-XXXX) for band members to view read-only confidence slides.

Usage Reporting & Automatic Backups

Automatic legal compliance and zero-panic data integrity.

STEP 6.1

Automatic Song Auditing

Every time a song is projected Live, Kairos records the song title, copyright metadata, author, and exact timestamp to the local usage log.

STEP 6.2

Export Usage CSV

When reporting time arrives, open Settings > Reports & Logs and click Download Song Usage CSV. The exported file matches the exact column format needed for church copyright and licensing reporting.

STEP 6.3

Automatic Daily Snapshots

Your library is safely saved after every action. Every day the app runs, it automatically saves a timestamped snapshot to backups/, ensuring you can never lose your library.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions before you switch

How does the dual-display output work with secondary monitors?

Kairos creates separate dedicated windows for the Sanctuary Projector and Stage Confidence Monitor. In the Settings > Displays menu, you simply select which physical display adapter receives which output. Once assigned, Kairos remembers the configuration and restores full screen automatically on launch.

Do I need an active internet connection on Sunday morning?

No. Kairos is built completely local-first. Your entire song library, Bible translations (31,102 verses), service plans, and presentation settings are stored locally on your own computer. Sunday services never fail because of church Wi-Fi dropouts.

Can worship leaders or musicians use an iPad as a wireless remote?

Yes! Kairos includes a built-in wireless remote feature. When enabled, anyone on the sanctuary's local Wi-Fi can scan the QR code to open the remote in Safari or Chrome. Operators can grant full Controller access or read-only Stage Monitor Viewer access.

Can a new volunteer run the service with zero prior training?

Yes. That is the core design principle behind the 3-zone switcher. Searching and clicking around never touches the projector. Staging a slide in Preview lets the volunteer double-check the lyrics privately. A single tap on the Spacebar takes it Live.

How does song usage and copyright reporting work?

Kairos tracks every song taken Live into a local usage log. At the end of the month or quarter, go to Settings > Reports & Logs and click "Download Song Usage CSV". The file format is directly organized for copyright compliance and song usage reporting.

Can I import our existing ChordPro and text song files?

Yes. Kairos includes high-speed importers for ChordPro (.cho, .chopro, .chordpro, .crd, .pro) and plain text lyrics. It automatically parses chords, detects song sections (Verse, Chorus, Bridge), and automatically detects duplicate songs.

What operating systems are supported?

Kairos runs natively on 64-bit Windows 10/11 and macOS (both Apple Silicon M1/M2/M3 and Intel). A native Linux build is scheduled for release following the Windows/macOS rollout.

What is your policy on software licenses and recurring subscriptions?

You pay once for a perpetual license for the major version purchased. There are no monthly subscriptions, no seat fees, and all minor feature updates and stability patches are provided free of charge.
